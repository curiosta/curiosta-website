A body-worn monitor for studies that link a participant's particulate exposure to where they are and what they are doing. Readings are logged every minute to an SD card, so the data can be lined up with a time-activity diary and used to estimate inhaled or deposited dose.

Choose the **SPS30** variant for PM mass concentrations, or the **OPC-N3** variant when you also need the particle size distribution. GPS and Wi-Fi/4G upload can be added for location tagging and remote data collection.
